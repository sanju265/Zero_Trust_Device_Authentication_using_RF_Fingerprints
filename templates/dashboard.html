Decision rule

How legitimacy is judged

TSGAE reconstruction

The Temporal Spectral Graph Autoencoder reconstructs the live RF feature window for each transmitter claim.

Profile distance

Mahalanobis distance checks whether the live RF feature window still matches that device's learned feature profile.

Bayesian Zero Trust

The dashboard compares the reconstruction ratio and the profile-distance ratio. The higher one becomes the decision ratio: below 0.85x stays legitimate, 0.85x-1.00x is treated as suspicious, and above 1.00x becomes an impersonation/RF mismatch.
